✦ A LETTER FROM YOUR PAGAL DOST ✦

Dear Baklolniiiii, ❤️

So sabse pehle, HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO MY DEAR BAKLOLNIIII, GANJICHURELLLL, PAGAL SI DOST! 😭❤️

Mujhe nahi pata main kya-kya likhunga, par haan, main bahut hi lucky hoon ki tumhare jaisi dost meri zindagi mein aayi.

Tumhare aane ke baad meri life pehle se poori change ho gayi hai. Pehle meri life mein kuch bhi hota tha, koi achhi baat ho ya buri, mere paas koi nahi tha jiske saath main woh sab share kar sakun. Kabhi kuch achha hota tha toh khushi apne tak hi reh jaati thi, aur kabhi kuch bura hota tha toh khud hi sab handle karna padta tha.

Par jab se tum aayi ho, sab kuch badal gaya hai. Ab kuch bhi hota hai toh dimaag mein sabse pehla naam tumhara aata hai. Koi achhi baat ho ya bura waqt, tum hamesha mere saath khadi rehti ho.

Pata hai, kuch log zindagi mein bas aate nahi, balki zindagi ko pehle se zyada khoobsurat bana jaate hain... aur tum unhi logon mein se ek ho. ❤️

Maine sapne mein bhi nahi socha tha ki kisi ke saath mera itna achha bond banega. Ek aisa bond jahan bina soche kuch bhi bol sakta hoon, bina dare apni problems share kar sakta hoon aur bina kisi wajah ke bhi has sakta hoon.

Main koi galti karun toh mujhe daantna, bilkul like my mom. Aur jab main koi chhoti-moti achievement karun, toh mujhse zyada tum khush hoti ho. Kabhi-kabhi toh lagta hai meri khushi se zyada excitement tumhe hoti hai. 😂❤️

Mujhe samjhana, meri illogical baaton ko bardasht karna aur meri faltu ki bakwaas sunna — ye har kisi ke bas ki baat nahi hai.

Ab toh ye sab daily routine ban gaya hai. Subah uthna, tumhe reels bhejna, rage bait karna, faltu ka drama karna, jo dil mein aaye bol dena aur meri meaningless baaton par meaningless advice dena. 😭

Aur sach kahun toh maine apni life mein tumse zyada pure-hearted insaan shayad kabhi dekha hi nahi. Tumhara nature, tumhari care karne ka tareeka aur bina kisi expectation ke apno ke liye khade rehna, ye sab tumhe bahut special banata hai.

Kabhi main kisi musibat mein hota hoon na, toh mere mann mein ek alag hi sukoon hota hai ki chalo koi nahi, meri ek dost toh hai. Woh mere support mein khadi hogi, milkar dekh lenge jo bhi hoga. Shayad tumhe iska andaaza bhi nahi hai ki tumhara hona mere liye kitni badi strength hai.

Aur haan, THANK YOU meri itni saari faltu reels par react karne ke liye. 😂 Waise meri tarah tum bhi kaafi veeli aur berozgaar ho, woh alag baat hai. 😭

Pata hai, humari friendship ki script bhi kamaal hai — na koi logic, na koi budget, bas faltu reels, attitude aur unlimited drama! 😂

Life mein bahut saare log milte hain, lekin har koi apni life ka itna important part nahi ban pata. Tum ab meri life ka ek bahut hi important hissa ban chuki ho. Just like a golden page in my book of life, jise main kabhi skip nahi karna chahunga.

Aur honestly, mujhe nahi lagta ki koi bhi kabhi tumhari jagah le sakta hai. Har insaan ki life mein kuch log aise hote hain jinki presence hi kaafi hoti hai, aur mere liye tum unhi logon mein se ek ho.

Shayad main har baar express nahi kar pata, ya kabhi mazaak-mazaak mein serious baatein bhi taal deta hoon, but I genuinely appreciate you. Tumhari friendship mere liye un cheezon mein se ek hai jiske liye main hamesha grateful rahunga.

Aur haan, tumhare CEE medical exam ke baare mein bhi ek baat kehna chahta hoon.

Mujhe pata hai tumne uske liye kitni mehnat ki thi, kitni hopes ke saath preparation ki aur kitna kuch sacrifice karke apna best dene ki koshish ki. Shayad result tumhari expectations ke according nahi aaya, lekin iska matlab bilkul nahi ki tumhari mehnat kam thi ya tum mein koi kami hai.

I just want you to remember ki main sirf tumhari achievements par proud nahi hoon, balki tumhari mehnat, dedication aur patience par bhi proud hoon. Ek exam ka result tumhari worth, capabilities ya tumhara future decide nahi kar sakta.

Zindagi ki picture mein ek exam ka result interval ho sakta hai, THE END nahi. Tumhari kahaani abhi bahut baaki hai!

Aur jab bhi kabhi tumhe lage ki cheezein difficult ho rahi hain, toh yaad rakhna ki tumhe har baar sab kuch akele handle karne ki zarurat nahi hai. Jaise tum mere liye khadi rehti ho, waise hi main bhi tumhare support mein hoon. We will figure things out, one step at a time.

Bas apne aap par bharosa kabhi mat khona. Mujhe tum par proud feel karne ke liye kisi result ya rank ki zarurat nahi hai. ❤️

And lastly, I just want to say THANK YOU.

Thank you for coming into my life, for being yourself, for listening to my nonsense, for understanding me and for making so many ordinary days special.

I don't know what the future holds for us, where life will take us or how busy we will become, but I genuinely hope ki humara ye bond kabhi change na ho.

Chahe kitne bhi saal beet jaayein, I hope hum aise hi ek dusre ko reels bhejte rahein, bina kisi baat ke ladte rahein, chat mein like wale emoji spam karte rahein, tum apna attitude dikhati raho aur main tumhara attitude jhelta rahun. 😂😭

Bas ye saari chhoti-chhoti pagalpan wali cheezein kabhi band na hon, kyunki honestly, inhi sab mein toh humari friendship ki asli khubsurati hai.

Agar meri life ek film hoti, toh tum definitely uska woh character hoti jiske bina poori story adhuri reh jaati. Aur yakeen maano, tumhare bina meri life ki script itni interesting kabhi nahi hoti. ❤️

I wish you all the happiness, success and love that you truly deserve. I hope ek din tum apne saare dreams achieve karo aur peeche mudkar dekho toh apni journey par proud feel karo.

I hope tumhari life mein itni khushiyan aayein ki tumhe kabhi apni smile ki wajah dhoondhni na pade. Tumhare saare dreams poore hon, tum apni pasand ki life jiyo aur hamesha khush raho.

Aur haan, kabhi ye mat bhoolna ki tumhari life mein ek pagal dost hai jo tumhari faltu baatein sunne, tumhe pareshan karne aur zarurat padne par tumhare saath khade rehne ke liye hamesha ready rahega. 😂❤️

I may not say it every day, but I'm genuinely grateful to have you in my life.

HAPPY BIRTHDAY ONCE AGAIN, MY DEAR BAKLOLNIIII! ❤️

Stay the same pagal, pure-hearted and wonderful person that you are. Kabhi khud ko kisi aur ki expectations ke hisaab se change karne ki zarurat mat samajhna.

And no matter how many pages my life has, I'm glad that one of its golden pages has your name written on it.

THANK YOU FOR BEING YOU. ❤️

With lots of duas & friendship,
Your Pagal Dost ❤️
